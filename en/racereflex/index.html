Race Reflex, the driver's reflex test

Five red lights come on. They go out. You react. Race Reflex measures your reaction time at the start, to the thousandth of a second, just like on a real grid.

Get it on Google Play

Free · Android

Features

World ranking

Five starts, the average of your three best server-checked times, and your place among all drivers.

Grand Prix and daily tournament

Events at a set time and a knockout bracket every day, with medals and steering wheels for the podium.

Race mode

Take the start, then complete your laps on circuits inspired by legendary tracks.

With friends

Duel for 2 to 4 drivers, relay up to 6, tournament up to 16 on one phone, or online challenges.

Your showcase

Helmets, medals and steering wheels you win are shown on your driver card.

Ranked Elo duels

Race online against drivers of your level: your Elo rating goes up or down after each duel.

Frequently asked questions

How is reaction time measured?

To the thousandth of a second, between lights out and your tap. Tapping too early counts as a false start.

Can several people play on one phone?

Yes: duel for 2 to 4 drivers, relay for 2 to 6, and tournament for 3 to 16 drivers.

Is the app free?

Race Reflex is free to download on Google Play. It may contain ads and in-app purchases.

Which languages?

English, French, Spanish, German and Italian.

Can I delete my ranking data?

Yes, at any time from the app settings.

Is Race Reflex linked to an official competition?

No. Race Reflex is an independent app, not affiliated with any motorsport competition organiser.
