Patrimoney, your wealth made clear

Property, investments, savings and loans: Patrimoney brings your whole net worth together, shows what it really earns and helps you plan ahead. No account to create, your figures stay on your phone.

Get it on Google Play

Free · Android

Features

Overview

Net worth, assets, debts and allocation by category, updated every month.

History

How your wealth evolves, separating what you saved from what your investments earned.

Fees and taxes

For each investment: yearly fees, their cost over 20 years and estimated tax if you sell.

Comparison

See where your household stands among French households of your age, based on INSEE data.

Score and tips

A score out of 100 and concrete pointers: emergency savings, debt load, diversification.

Projections

Your net worth in 10 or 20 years, in today's euros, based on your contributions.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Patrimoney works without an account or sign-up.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. You stay in control of your figures and their backup.

Is the app free?

Patrimoney is free to download on Google Play. It may contain ads and in-app purchases.

Does Patrimoney replace an adviser?

No. Scores, calculations and projections are estimates for information only. They are not investment, tax or legal advice.

Which devices does Patrimoney run on?

Android phones, through Google Play.
