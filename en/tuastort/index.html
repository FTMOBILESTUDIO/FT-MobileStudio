FT Mobile Studio / Tu as tort

Tu as tort, the app that is never wrong

“Tu as tort” means “you're wrong”. Type any statement, even the most obvious one. It finds the counterexample that flips it and reads it out loud, with infuriating calm.

Get it on Google Play

Free · Android · In French · iPhone coming soon

Features

It contradicts everything

Even “two plus two is four”. It always finds an angle: a real counterexample or, failing that, shameless bad faith.

Facts, not fiction

Every answer looks for a true, precise example. No made-up figures, nothing hurtful.

Hear it

A deep, composed voice reads every answer, with a smug smile.

The debate goes on

Add your “why” and try again: it finds another angle. It will never admit you're right.

Share your defeats

Send a screenshot of an answer to your friends so they find out why they're wrong too.

Type or dictate

Type your statement or dictate it with the keyboard mic.

Frequently asked questions

Is the app free?

Yes. Up to 10 answers a day: 6 free, then 2 more for each short video watched (2 videos max). The app shows ads.

Can it ever say I'm right?

Never. That's the whole point.

Are the answers reliable?

It looks for a real counterexample; when there is none, it goes for a bit of shameless bad faith. It's a game: never take its answers as advice.

Which language does it speak?

French only for now.

Is it available on iPhone?

Soon. It is already available on Android, on Google Play.
